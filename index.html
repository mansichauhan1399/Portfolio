What to show

Lead with 3–4 high-value healthcare product stories.

Prioritize AI Agents for Outreach, Strategy AI Workflows + UI Builder, Medication Review Revamp, and a combined SMS + EHR Writeback story. These show range: AI, workflow design, clinical complexity, systems thinking, and end-to-end ownership.

College projects?

Include one only if it reveals a different superpower.

If a college project shows research, visual craft, service design, or a domain outside healthcare, place it after professional work. Keep it short and frame it as “where my product-thinking muscle started.”

Storytelling formula: everyday problem → why it mattered → my role → key decisions → simple before/after → measurable or qualitative impact → what I learned.

Made for a portfolio that feels warm, sharp, and a little unexpected.
